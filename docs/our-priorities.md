# Children’s rights, at the heart of public life

These starting themes describe the world we want to help build. They are an invitation to discussion, rather than an adopted policy platform.

## A voice that counts

Children should have opportunities to express their views, ask questions, and be taken seriously in decisions that affect them. Participation should be accessible, age-appropriate, and voluntary.

## Room to grow

Every child should have space to learn, play, rest, and discover who they are. Safe homes, supportive communities, and access to care are central to that vision.

## A world to inherit

Children deserve a peaceful, livable world today and in the future. Our shared choices should account for the people who will live with their consequences.
