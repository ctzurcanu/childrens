# Individual and Community

1. (Most of) The individual's rights are the result of the duties of the community.
2. The individual's duties are the result of the rights of the community.
3. The rights of the community are the result of the individual duties.
4. The duties of the community are the result of the individual rights.

## Mappings

### Individual Rights -> Community Duties

1. Existence: 
  - Medical Emergency Services
  - Identity Management Services
  - Homeless Shelters
2. Autonomy:
  - Language, Writing System
  - Rule of Law
  - Public Roads, Rules of the Way
  - Public Transport
  - Public Parks
  - Public Education
  - Markets
  - Currency guarantee
  - Charities
  - Police
  - Public Signs
  - Organized Labour: Enterprises, Syndicates
  - Internet Services
  - Other Institutions
  - Political Representation
3. Justice
  - Access to Justice
  - Voting
  - Tax collection
  - Budget management


### Individual Duties -> Community Rights

1. To Belong in a Community and not in another
  - Existence
  - Identity
2. To offer services to a Community
  - Autonomy
3. To accept evidence/mediation and to mediate. To vote.
  - Justice
