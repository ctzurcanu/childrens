# Children's Duties

We should always start with our duties first, before asking for farness, justice, or rights.


0. Adopt the [Children's International meta-ethos](/ideals/meta-ethos.md)
1. (for additional rights): Adopting an Ethos that is compatible with Children's International meta-ethos
1. Clear and Provable communication
1. Defending those who/that seem defenseless
1. [Political Xenia](./xenia.md) — Bring the interests of those who cannot or do not wish to speak for themselves into our decisions.
