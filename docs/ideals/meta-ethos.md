## Declarations

### The Golden Rule: Negative

- short: golden_rule_negative
- long: WE do not do to others what I do not wish done to myself.

### Only one Ethos

- short: one_ethos
- long: We have only one meta-ethos, made public here.

### Shared Ethos

- short: shared_ethos
- long: What we believe, we share in this ethos.

### Acknowledge the Existence of Another Ethos

- short: ack_other_ethos
- long: We acknowledge the existence of other ethos(es) than ours.

### Other Ethos of Same Importance

- short: ack_other_ethos_importance
- long: We acknowledge that, unless proven otherwise, other ethos(es) than ours have an equal importance for third parties to our ethos.

### No Contradiction

- short: no_contradiction_claim
- long: We will keep our meta-ethos and ethos free of contradictions.
