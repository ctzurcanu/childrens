# Make room for every voice

These principles describe the discussion space we want to build.

- **Listen with respect.** Discuss ideas without attacking people. No bullying, discrimination, or harassment.
- **Protect privacy.** Do not post addresses, school details, phone numbers, or private information about yourself or others.
- **Keep participation voluntary.** Nobody needs to share a personal story or identifying details to be heard.
- **Take care with claims.** Distinguish experiences, opinions, and facts. Share sources when useful.
- **Make space.** Use clear language and welcome different perspectives and experiences.

Our public discussion space has not yet launched. Before opening it, we will need clear moderation arrangements, reporting routes, and participation requirements. Follow the rules and age requirements of any external service linked from this site.
