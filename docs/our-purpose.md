# A world that listens to children

**Our lives. Our voices. Our world.**

The Children’s International is a political party that defends the rights of children everywhere.

Children are part of public life. Decisions about schools, neighborhoods, the environment, and peace shape their everyday experiences and their futures. Their perspectives deserve a place in those decisions.

## What brings us together

Our starting point is simple: every child matters. We want a world where children can grow in safety, learn, play, and be heard, wherever they live.

## Building our direction

This site is a starting point for the movement. The themes on our [priorities page](./our-priorities.md) introduce that purpose; they are not an adopted policy platform. Detailed policies and ways to participate will be shared as they develop.
