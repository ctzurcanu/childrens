# Ideals worth our Fight

## Children's Duties

We should always start with our duties first, before asking for justice or rights.

1. Clear and Provable communication
2. Adopting an Ethos that is compatible with Children's International meta-ethos
3. Defending those that seem defenseless
4. [Political Xenia](./xenia.md) — Bring the interests of those who cannot or do not wish to speak for themselves into our decisions.


## Children’s Rights

Children live with decisions made by adult-elected leaders, yet they have no say in choosing them.

These are proposals for children's rights:

0. [Independent Ethos](./your-own-ethos.md) — Develop your values, explain them, and change them as you learn.
1. [Understanding an election before voting](./voting-comprehension.md) — A proposed way for children to show they understand the choices in an election.
2. [Keeping evidence and asking for help](./keeping-evidence.md) — Save information about harm and report it without punishment.
3. [An online community for children](./children-only-social-network.md) — Make friends and run online spaces without adults watching every conversation.
4. [Your money belongs to you](./owning-and-earning-money.md) — Own money, earn income, and control a bank account at any age.
5. [A fair jury that includes children](./jury-of-peers.md) — Give children an equal part in deciding disputes involving children and adults.
7. [The freedom to travel](./freedom-to-travel.md) — Travel independently, with help when wanted and fair ways to challenge restrictions.
8. [Making agreements in your own name](./signing-contracts.md) — Make clear agreements and ask for promises to be kept.
9. [Privacy, a safe box, and a safe space](./privacy-and-safe-spaces.md) — Keep personal things private and have somewhere safe to go.
10. [Voting and becoming an elected leader](./voting-and-being-elected.md) — Vote, run for election, and do the real job if elected, at any age, with understanding checks for children.
