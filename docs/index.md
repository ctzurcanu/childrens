# The Children's International

## What is it?

It could be:

- an international political party
- a meta-organization
- a place for friendship
- a way to help other children
- the hope and imagination transformed into goodness
- a world for children


## What it ought not to be?

- a charity where children beg for resources
- a system for adults to surveil or control children
