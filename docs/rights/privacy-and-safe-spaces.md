# Privacy, a safe box, and a safe space

**The idea: children should have privacy, secure storage for their belongings, and places where they can be alone or find safety.**

This is a proposed right. It describes changes we could make, not a guide to today’s laws.

## Private messages and belongings

Parents and institutions would not automatically have the right to read a child’s messages, diary, or letters, or demand passwords. Wanting privacy would not itself be treated as proof of wrongdoing.

A **safe box** could be a locked physical compartment or secure digital storage. Children could keep documents, money, evidence, personal belongings, and creative work there. Adults would not automatically receive spare keys or a way to reset access.

## Room to be yourself

Children would have somewhere to change clothes, rest, think, and talk privately without routine watching, recording, or uninvited entry. Shared homes would still need ways to provide privacy.

A **place of refuge** is somewhere you can go to be safe. Children could reach a place outside the control of someone threatening or harming them, without needing that person’s permission.

A safe space must be voluntary. It must not become somewhere adults lock a child in.

## What about emergencies?

Entering a private space or accessing private information would require a concrete danger. The action would have to be limited to what is necessary, explained, and open to independent review. Caring for a child would not give someone permission to watch them all the time.

## An example

A child shares a bedroom. Under this proposal, they would still have a lockable place for their diary and arrangements for changing clothes privately. If home or school is unsafe, independent storage and a voluntary place of refuge would be available.

## Questions still to answer

- How could shared housing provide real privacy?
- How would children reach independent storage and places of refuge?
- How could someone recover access after losing a key or password without giving adults automatic access?
- Exactly which emergencies would justify an intrusion, and who would review it?
